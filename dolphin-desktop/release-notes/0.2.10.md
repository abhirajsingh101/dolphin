Dolphin 0.2.10 makes full screen and downloads easier to follow.

- **The top bar works in full screen.** A fullscreen terminal window now
  sits below the top bar. The bell's list and Downloads open over it, and
  the projects list and chat come back beside it when you show them.
  Leaving full screen restores your layout.
- **You see a download start.** An arrow drops into the Downloads button
  and a ring spreads from it, as in a browser. Nothing moves when your
  system asks for reduced motion.

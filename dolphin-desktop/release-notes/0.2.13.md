Dolphin 0.2.13 gives every project its own character.

- **Eight characters.** Pip, Mochi, Bolt, Ember, Luna, Pebble, Fizz and
  Nori replace the simple shapes. Each is drawn by hand and moves like a
  character: it blinks, breathes, follows your pointer, squashes when you
  pick it up, lands with a bounce, and acts each mood. It hops when work
  finishes, leans in when something needs you, and thinks while its chat
  answers. It keeps still while you type in a terminal, and with reduced
  motion it stays still.
- **Picked for each project.** Dolphin chooses a character from what is in
  the project folder: notebooks get Nori, writing gets Mochi, apps get
  Ember, and so on. Click the character at the top of the Tasks tab to
  choose another, or set it back to Automatic.
